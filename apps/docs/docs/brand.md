The EUDIPLO logo files and colors, for slides, articles and project listings that mention EUDIPLO.

## Diplo

The logo is Diplo, a diplodocus, on a navy badge with a yellow sun. The first half of _Diplodocus_ comes from the Greek _diplóos_, meaning "double". Diplo borrows that idea for the two things EUDIPLO helps organizations do: issue credentials and request presentations.

A university can issue a student credential to a wallet. Later, a museum can ask for proof of student status, and the student can choose to share it. EUDIPLO can power either side of that story.

## Colors

| ------------- | --------- |
| Badge         | `#1E1C4B` |
| Diplo         | `#A0A2F7` |
| Neck shadow   | `#6266F0` |
| Belly and eye | `#FFFFFF` |
| Sun           | `#FFC53B` |

The wordmark in the lockups is `#1B1B1F` on light backgrounds and `#E5E1E6` on dark ones. The accent color for text and lines is `#964900` on light backgrounds and `#FFB787` on dark ones. The Web Client builds its Material 3 theme from `#343DFF` (primary) and `#964900` (tertiary).
