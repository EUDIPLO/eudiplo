## Diplo

The logo is Diplo, a diplodocus. The first half of _Diplodocus_ comes from the Greek _diplóos_, meaning "double". Diplo borrows that idea for the two things EUDIPLO helps organizations do: issue credentials and request presentations.

A university can issue a student credential to a wallet. Later, a museum can ask for proof of student status, and the student can choose to share it. EUDIPLO can power either side of that story.

The shape on Diplo's back is the credential itself. Its folded corner is a second color in the color versions and a cut-out in the single-color version.
