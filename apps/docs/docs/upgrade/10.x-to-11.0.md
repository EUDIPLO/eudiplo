# Upgrading from 10.x to 11.0

Each section below says what changed in EUDIPLO 11.0 and what to do; skip the sections that do not apply to you.

## Before you upgrade

- Upgrade from any 10.x release. On 9.x or older, [upgrade to 10.x first](./index.md#upgrade-guides).
- If you verify credentials, check that the CA certificates of their issuers' chains, and of their status list signers' chains, carry `basicConstraints` `cA` TRUE, and that no certificate marks an extension critical that EUDIPLO does not process, such as `nameConstraints`: such credentials are now rejected ([Presentations: certificate paths](#presentations-certificate-paths-must-meet-rfc-5280-constraints)). This includes credentials you issue yourself under a certificate from your own PKI.
- Follow the [upgrade procedure](./index.md#upgrade-procedure).

## Operators

### Presentations: certificate paths must meet RFC 5280 constraints

**Changed:** a presented SD-JWT VC or mDOC is rejected with `no_trust_chain_to_root`, HTTP 400 to the wallet and session `failed`, when the certificate path from its signing certificate up to the certificate the trust list lists breaks an RFC 5280 constraint: a certificate that issues the one below it must be a CA (`basicConstraints` `cA` TRUE, `keyCertSign`, `pathLenConstraint`), and no certificate may mark an extension critical that EUDIPLO does not process, such as `nameConstraints`. This includes the certificate the trust list lists, when it issued the one below it, and version 1 or 2 CA certificates, which carry no `basicConstraints`. Wallets get HTTP 400 for such credentials. The same applies to the path of its status list or identifier list: an SD-JWT VC then fails with `Status List JWT verification failed`, an mDOC with `no_trust_chain_to_root`, also with `statusCheckMode: best_effort`. Without a trust list (`SKIP_TRUST_AUTHORITY=true`), an mDOC's presented chain is checked in the same way. 10.x checked only signatures, validity periods and the match with the trust list: any end-entity key of a listed provider could issue further signing certificates, and a critical extension with a restriction EUDIPLO does not enforce was ignored. The certificates of ISO/IEC 18013-5 Annex B and those EUDIPLO creates for its key chains meet the constraints. Wallet and key attestations are unchanged.

**Do:** nothing for issuers whose CA certificates carry `basicConstraints` `cA` TRUE and mark only the extensions listed in [Certificate path constraints](../trust/trust-lists.md#certificate-path-constraints) critical. If presentations fail with `no_trust_chain_to_root` and the server log says `Certificate path constraint: ...`, ask the issuer, or for a status list its signer, for a certificate chain that meets the constraints; the message names the certificate and the constraint. If you issue under a key chain imported from your own PKI, check its certificates the same way. Details: [Certificate path constraints](../trust/trust-lists.md#certificate-path-constraints).
