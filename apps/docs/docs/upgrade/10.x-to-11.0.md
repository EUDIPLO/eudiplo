# Upgrading from 10.x to 11.0

Each section below says what changed in EUDIPLO 11.0 and what to do; skip the sections that do not apply to you.

## Before you upgrade

- Upgrade from any 10.x release. On 9.x or older, [upgrade to 10.x first](./index.md#upgrade-guides).
- If you accept wallet or key attestations in the TS3 1.5 format (EUDI Wallet), check that every wallet provider serves the status lists their `client_status` and `key_storage_status` name and signs them as its other status lists: they are now checked ([Wallet and key attestations](#wallet-and-key-attestations-ts3-15-status-claims-are-checked)).
- Follow the [upgrade procedure](./index.md#upgrade-procedure).

## Operators

### Wallet and key attestations: TS3 1.5 status claims are checked

**Changed:** EUDIPLO reads the status list entry of a wallet attestation from `client_status.status` and of a key attestation from `key_storage_status.status`, the claims of TS3 (EUDI Wallet Unit Attestations) 1.5 and later, in addition to the top-level `status` claim of OpenID4VCI 1.0 and TS3 1.4.1. 10.x ignored the TS3 claims, so it accepted attestations of revoked wallet instances and key storages and never fetched their lists. With `walletAttestationStatusCheckMode` `strict` or `best_effort` (the default), such an attestation is now rejected as one with a top-level `status` is: when its entry is revoked or suspended, when its list cannot be read, is not the named list or is not signed by the provider, and when the claim is malformed (`client_status` or `key_storage_status` not a JSON object, or a malformed `status` in it). `strict` also rejects it when the list cannot be fetched. An attestation that carries both claims has both entries checked. The PAR and token endpoints (including the refresh grant) answer as for a top-level `status` claim, the credential endpoint `invalid_proof`. With `revokeWithWalletUnit`, a credential issued for a TS3 1.5 key attestation now follows its `key_storage_status` entry ([Which claim names the status](../trust/attestation.md#which-claim-names-the-status)); credentials issued by 10.x keep following the entry recorded then.

**Do:** before you upgrade, check with your wallet providers that the lists their TS3 attestations name are served and signed as their other status lists: under the revocation certificate your wallet-provider trust lists name for the provider, or its attestation certificate if they name none. To accept attestations while a list is unreachable, keep `best_effort`; `disabled` skips the check.
